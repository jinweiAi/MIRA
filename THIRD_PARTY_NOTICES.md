# Third-party components

MIRA imports Qwen2.5-VL through Hugging Face Transformers. The decoder integration
follows Qwen2.5-VL's Q/K/V, grouped-query attention, multimodal RoPE, residual and
MLP ordering. Qwen2.5-VL and Transformers are distributed under Apache-2.0.

- Qwen2.5-VL: https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen2.5-VL
- Transformers: https://github.com/huggingface/transformers
- PEFT: https://github.com/huggingface/peft
- PyTorch: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch

Those projects retain their respective copyrights and licenses. Their code and
model weights are not bundled here. Obtain model weights and datasets from their
original distributors and follow their applicable licenses and access terms.
